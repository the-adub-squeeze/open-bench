# Open Bench

build an application that crowd-sources donations for a free makers space that my company will own but will never charge for, strictly donation based. What should be clear to the user:

Their money goes straight to the purchase/lease of the building and is trackable in the sense that if we reach our goal they will see exactly how that money works for the community (almost like bitcoin but for public good, so they don’t think it goes into my pocket)

Upon purchase or lease of said space they will, anyone will actually, have the ability to bring their tools, materials, etc to the space and if we crowd source some lockers there welcome to lock them up at the end of the day. The target dollar amount is TBD bc I believe the community should get a say where it is: a large unused shed, an abandoned building or house bc hopefully that gets the community to help find places like this, for example they have an uncle with a heated garage he never uses etc. or something bigger. So that the space grows with the community
